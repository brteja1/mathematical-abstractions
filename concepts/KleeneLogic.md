# Strong Kleene three-valued logic (K3)

A truth-functional propositional semantics with false, unknown and true, strong Kleene connectives, and only true designated.

**Notation:** K={F,U,T}, F<U<T; ¬F=T, ¬U=U, ¬T=F; a∧b=min(a,b); a∨b=max(a,b); D={T}.

**Stable ID:** `urn:csabs:concept/KleeneLogic`

**Related concepts:** [Propositional Logic](PropositionalLogic.md), [Boolean algebra](BooleanAlgebra.md)

## Mathematical formulation

K={F,U,T}, F<U<T; ¬F=T, ¬U=U, ¬T=F; a∧b=min(a,b); a∨b=max(a,b); D={T}. K has exactly three distinct values and exact equality. Unknown is a truth-value gap, not a probability. All connectives are total mathematical functions, without evaluation-order or short-circuit effects. Formula syntax is finite and freely generated by atoms, bottom, negation and conjunction; disjunction is definable by De Morgan and implication is the abbreviation ¬p∨q. Γ⊨ₖ₃φ means every valuation designating all premises designates φ; the empty-premise case is validity. Weak Kleene logic, LP designation and Kleene-star algebras are different profiles. Classical excluded middle is not assumed. Utility profile: Truth-order min fold with identity T: all([])=T; all(a::xs)=a∧all(xs).; Truth-order max fold with identity F: any([])=F; any(a::xs)=a∨any(xs).
