# First-order formula syntax with named variables

Finite raw first-order formulas over a single-sorted signature, using the existing term algebra for terms and named quantifier binders. Free variables and capture-free substitution are syntactic interfaces.

**Notation:** t∈T_F(V); φ ::= R(t₁,…,tₙ) | t=u | ⊥ | ¬φ | φ∧ψ | ∀x φ; ∃x φ:=¬∀x¬φ

**Stable ID:** `urn:csabs:concept/FirstOrderFormula`

**Categories:** Logic and proof

**Kind:** Abstract data type

### substitute

`urn:csabs:operation/FirstOrderFormula-substitute`

**signature:** subTerm:Term×Term×V→Term; subFormula:Form×Term×V⇀Form

**description:** Replaces free x occurrences by t, stopping below a binder for x. Terms reuse TermAlgebra with variables V and function symbols F.

**precondition:** For subFormula, t is free for x in φ: no free x occurrence lies under a binder for a variable of t.

**totality:** subTerm is total; subFormula is defined exactly on the stated free-for domain. No binder renaming is implicit.

**outcome:** Replaces free x occurrences by t, stopping below a binder for x. Terms reuse TermAlgebra with variables V and function symbols F.

### free-syntax

`urn:csabs:axiom/FirstOrderFormula-free-syntax`

**statement:** Finite formulas have disjoint, injective primitive constructors and structural induction.

**formalStatement:** Form is the least set generated by the displayed grammar; constructor forms are disjoint and injective; a property true of atomic/equality/bottom forms and preserved by negation, conjunction and each ∀x holds for all formulas.

**assumptions:** V is a countably infinite variable supply; symbols have the arities in FirstOrderSignature. Raw tree equality includes binder names. Alpha equivalence and semantic equivalence are distinct relations. Substitution φ[t/x] is partial: t must be free for x in φ; bound renaming may prepare a representative but is not silently performed. Sentences have FV=∅.

### substitution-support

`urn:csabs:theorem/FirstOrderFormula-substitution-support`

**statement:** Capture-free substitution has precisely the expected free-variable support.

**formalStatement:** FV(φ[t/x])=(FV(φ)∖{x}) ∪ (vars(t) if x∈FV(φ) else ∅)

**assumptions:** V is a countably infinite variable supply; symbols have the arities in FirstOrderSignature. Raw tree equality includes binder names. Alpha equivalence and semantic equivalence are distinct relations. Substitution φ[t/x] is partial: t must be free for x in φ; bound renaming may prepare a representative but is not silently performed. Sentences have FV=∅.; t is free for x in φ.

**reviewStatus:** Editorial source and mathematical interface review; no compiled proof witness asserted.

**notationLanguage:** informal mathematical Unicode

**dependsOn:** [free-syntax](FirstOrderFormula.md#node-urn-csabs-axiom-FirstOrderFormula-free-syntax); [free-variables](FirstOrderFormula.md#node-urn-csabs-axiom-FirstOrderFormula-free-variables); [substitution](FirstOrderFormula.md#node-urn-csabs-axiom-FirstOrderFormula-substitution); [induction](TermAlgebra.md#node-urn-csabs-axiom-TermAlgebra-induction)

**proofSketch:** First prove the analogous term statement by induction. Then induct on φ. At ∀y with y≠x and x free in its body, the free-for condition excludes y from vars(t), permitting removal of y to commute with the union.
