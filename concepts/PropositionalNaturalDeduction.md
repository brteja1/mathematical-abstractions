# Classical propositional natural deduction

The least derivability relation generated by finite assumption-discharge proof trees for classical propositional formulas, with contexts treated as sets.

**Notation:** Γ⊢p; Γ⊆F(A); primitive rules for ⊥, ¬, ∧ plus classical reductio

**Stable ID:** `urn:csabs:concept/PropositionalNaturalDeduction`

**Categories:** Logic and proof

**Kind:** Mathematical structure

**Prerequisites:** [Finite propositional formula syntax](PropositionalFormula.md), [Propositional Logic](PropositionalLogic.md)

## Interface and scope

**axiomBasis:** Least finitary derivability relation with assumption, weakening, ∧ introduction/projections, ¬ introduction/elimination, explosion and classical reductio; ∨, ⇒, ↔ and ⊤ are macros.

**basisKind:** inductive-definition

**basisScope:** Finite proof trees; contexts may be infinite, but proofs use finitely many assumptions. Exchange/contraction are implicit in set contexts; weakening is permitted. Connective macros expand before applying primitive rules. Countable atom supply for the stated strong completeness and compactness results; soundness holds for any atom set.

### rules

`urn:csabs:axiom/PropositionalNaturalDeduction-rules`

**statement:** Assumptions, weakening, conjunction, negation and explosion generate derivations.

**formalStatement:** φ∈Γ ⇒ Γ⊢φ; (Γ⊢φ ∧ Γ⊆Δ)⇒Δ⊢φ; Γ⊢φ,Γ⊢ψ / Γ⊢φ∧ψ; Γ⊢φ∧ψ / Γ⊢φ and Γ⊢ψ; Γ∪{φ}⊢⊥ / Γ⊢¬φ; Γ⊢φ,Γ⊢¬φ / Γ⊢⊥; Γ⊢⊥ / Γ⊢φ. The relation is the least closure under these and the classical rule.

**assumptions:** Finite proof trees; contexts may be infinite, but proofs use finitely many assumptions. Exchange/contraction are implicit in set contexts; weakening is permitted. ### completeness

`urn:csabs:theorem/PropositionalNaturalDeduction-completeness`

**statement:** Every semantic consequence has a finite classical natural deduction proof.

**formalStatement:** Γ⊨φ ⇒ Γ⊢φ

**assumptions:** A is at most countable; Γ is any set of finite formulas over A.; Classical metatheory; the recorded least, finitary derivability relation, including reductio.

**reviewStatus:** Editorial source and mathematical interface review; no compiled proof witness asserted.

**notationLanguage:** informal mathematical Unicode

**dependsOn:** [rules](PropositionalNaturalDeduction.md#node-urn-csabs-axiom-PropositionalNaturalDeduction-rules); [classical-reductio](PropositionalNaturalDeduction.md#node-urn-csabs-axiom-PropositionalNaturalDeduction-classical-reductio); [truth-semantics](PropositionalLogic.md#node-urn-csabs-axiom-PropositionalLogic-truth-semantics); [entailment](PropositionalLogic.md#node-urn-csabs-axiom-PropositionalLogic-entailment)

**proofSketch:** If Γ does not derive φ, classical reductio makes Γ∪{¬φ} consistent. Enumerate formulas and extend to a complete consistent set; assign each atom its membership truth value. Structural induction gives the truth lemma, yielding a countervaluation. The minimal basis simulates the displayed full connective rules by expanding the macros.

### compactness

`urn:csabs:theorem/PropositionalNaturalDeduction-compactness`

**statement:** Satisfiability is determined by finite subsets.

**formalStatement:** sat(Γ) ⇔ ∀finite Γ₀⊆Γ,sat(Γ₀)

**assumptions:** At most countably many atoms; arbitrary premise set Γ.; Classical two-valued semantics and the recorded finitary proof system.

**reviewStatus:** Editorial source and mathematical interface review; no compiled proof witness asserted.

**notationLanguage:** informal mathematical Unicode

**dependsOn:** [finite-support](PropositionalNaturalDeduction.md#node-urn-csabs-law-PropositionalNaturalDeduction-finite-support); [soundness](PropositionalNaturalDeduction.md#node-urn-csabs-theorem-PropositionalNaturalDeduction-soundness); [completeness](PropositionalNaturalDeduction.md#node-urn-csabs-theorem-PropositionalNaturalDeduction-completeness); [semantic-queries](PropositionalLogic.md#node-urn-csabs-axiom-PropositionalLogic-semantic-queries); [entailment](PropositionalLogic.md#node-urn-csabs-axiom-PropositionalLogic-entailment)

**proofSketch:** If Γ is unsatisfiable it entails ⊥. Completeness produces a proof, finite support extracts Γ₀, and soundness makes Γ₀ unsatisfiable. The reverse direction restricts any satisfying valuation.
