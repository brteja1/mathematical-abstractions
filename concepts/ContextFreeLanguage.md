# Context-free language over a finite alphabet

A language of finite words generated by at least one context-free grammar with finitely many nonterminals and productions over the same finite alphabet.

**Notation:** CFL(Σ)={L⊆Σ* | ∃ finite CFG G over Σ, L=L(G)}

**Stable ID:** `urn:csabs:concept/ContextFreeLanguage`

**Categories:** Languages and computation

**Kind:** Mathematical structure

**Prerequisites:** [Formal language of finite words](FormalLanguage.md), [Context-free grammar of finite words](ContextFreeGrammar.md)

**Specializes:** [Formal language of finite words](FormalLanguage.md)

**Related concepts:** [Regular language over a finite alphabet](RegularLanguage.md)

## Mathematical formulation

ContextFree(L) ⇔ ∃ G=(N,Σ,P,S), finite(N) ∧ finite(P) ∧ S∈N ∧ P⊆N×(N+Σ)\* ∧ ∀w∈Σ\*, (w∈L ⇔ [S]⇒\*\_P terminal(w)).

**Lean formulation** (in its source module context):

```lean
def ContextFreeLanguage {A : Type} [Fintype A] (L : Language A) : Prop :=
  ∃ g : Catalog.ContextFreeGrammar A, g.grammar.language = L
```

**Source:** [`Catalog.ContextFreeLanguage`](../lean/Catalog/LanguageClasses.lean); consulted 2026-11-20.

**Scope:** model

**Interpretation:** ContextFree(L) ⇔ ∃ G=(N,Σ,P,S), finite(N) ∧ finite(P) ∧ S∈N ∧ P⊆N×(N+Σ)* ∧ ∀w∈Σ*, (w∈L ⇔ [S]⇒*_P terminal(w)).

**Limits:** Finite fixed alphabet, possibly empty; exact finite-word equality and extensional language equality. Existential witnesses have finite nonterminal sets, a start nonterminal, and finitely many single-nonterminal productions with finite right sides. Terminal and nonterminal symbols are disjoint tagged carriers. Empty productions, ambiguity, left recursion and nonproductive grammars are permitted. A language is not a chosen grammar; no parser, normal form, uniqueness or decision procedure is specified. Selected mathematical profile only; no blanket software refinement, missing closure theorem or automata-equivalence proof. Verification requires fresh compiler and axiom-audit evidence.
