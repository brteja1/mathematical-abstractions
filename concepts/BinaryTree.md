# Finite ordered binary tree with node labels

A finite rooted binary tree whose actual nodes carry labels in A and have ordered left and right child positions, each possibly empty. Values are freely generated by an empty tree E and a node constructor N(a,l,r).

**Notation:** BT(A)=μX.(1+A×X×X); t ::= E | N(a,t_left,t_right).

## Mathematical formulation

BT(A) is the least solution T=1+A×T×T: empty or node(a,l,r). fold(z,k,empty)=z; fold(z,k,node(a,l,r))=k(a,fold(z,k,l),fold(z,k,r)). Equality retains labels, shape and left/right order.

```lean
abbrev BinaryTree (A : Type u) := _root_.BinaryTree A
```

**Source:** [`Catalog.BinaryTree`](../lean/Catalog/BinaryTree.lean); consulted 2026-11-12.

**Scope:** model

**Interpretation:** Catalog.BinaryTree A is the pinned Mathlib BinaryTree A, freely generated by nil and node a l r. Product signatures are curried in Lean. E=empty=nil; node labels/ordered subtrees use Lean equality. view uses Option of the right-associated triple (a,(l,r)). size=numNodes; externalLeaves=numLeaves counts nil occurrences; height(nil)=0 and a singleton has height 1. fold, mirror and inorder are explicit recursive wrappers; map is Mathlib label mapping. A may be empty. Functions are pure/total and counts exact Nat; no search-order, balance, pointer identity or sharing observations.

**Limits:** Selected finite ordered node-labelled datatype only. Conversion to the undirected graph Tree is an editorial occurrence-graph instantiation, not a registered Lean theorem. Concrete mutable/shared/cyclic implementations require a refinement mapping.

## Interface and scope

**axiomBasis:** Free finite inductive carrier with empty and node constructors, disjointness/injectivity, and structural induction; tagged constructor view and fold computation rules; recursive definitions for size, height, external leaves, inorder, mirror and label mapping.

**basisKind:** inductive-data-type

**basisScope:** Finite, well-founded, rooted, ordered, node-labelled values, including E; A may be empty. Structural equality uses the chosen equality of A. No BST ordering, balance, key uniqueness, pointer identity, cyclic/infinite values or observable sharing is assumed. Operations are pure mathematical functions, counts use unbounded naturals, height(E)=0 and height(singleton)=1. External leaves count E occurrences, with externalLeaves(E)=1.

**basisInstantiation:** StructuralInduction is instantiated with constructors E and N:A×BT(A)×BT(A)→BT(A), giving hypotheses for exactly the left and right recursive positions. No axioms are inherited through relatedConcept or prerequisites.

**specializationNote:** Not a specialization of the existing nonempty undirected Tree carrier: E is permitted and equality retains labels and left/right order. For t≠E, take vertices as labelled-node occurrences addressed by finite L/R words and connect each nonroot occurrence to its parent; forgetting labels, root and child order instantiates Tree. Repeated equal labels remain distinct vertices; nonroot undirected degree may be 3.
